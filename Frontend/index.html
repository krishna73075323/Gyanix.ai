How Gyanix AI Works

From submission to resolution — AI handles everything in the middle.

01

Student Submits Report

Fill a simple anonymous form — choose your language (Hindi/English/Urdu), select issue type, and describe what happened. No login required.

02

Azure OpenAI Analyzes

GPT-4o processes the complaint in milliseconds — detecting category, severity, sentiment, and potential fake flags with high accuracy.

03

Admin Gets Priority View

Dashboard shows all cases sorted by AI severity. Critical cases are highlighted in red — admins can filter, review, and act immediately.

04

Case Resolved

Admin takes action, updates case status. Student can track their report status using the unique anonymous tracking ID provided at submission.
